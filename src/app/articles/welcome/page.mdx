Welcome to my running blog! I've been wanting to start documenting my running journey for a while now, and I'm excited to finally have a place to share my experiences.

## What to Expect

This blog will be home to:

- **Race Reports** - Detailed recaps of races I run, from 5Ks to marathons and beyond
- **Training Insights** - What's working, what's not, and lessons learned along the way
- **Gear Reviews** - Honest takes on shoes, watches, and other running gear
- **Running Life** - The ups, downs, and everything in between

## Why Blog About Running?

Running is more than just putting one foot in front of the other. Every run tells a story, and every race is an adventure. I've found that writing about my experiences helps me process them, learn from them, and hopefully inspire others along the way.

## Let's Go

Thanks for stopping by. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or just starting your running journey, I hope you find something here that resonates with you.

See you on the roads!
