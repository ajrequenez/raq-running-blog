I've been thinking about starting this blog for a while. Long enough that it stopped being an idea and started feeling like something I was avoiding. So here it is—the first post, the first step, the first mile on the page.

This blog is a place to document my running journey as it actually happens. Not just the highlight reels, not just the race photos, but the full picture: training runs that go well, runs that don't, mindset shifts, setbacks, and the slow work of getting healthier and stronger over time.

I'm aiming big. I want to run a full marathon next year. Saying that out loud—or writing it down—makes it feel real. And a little uncomfortable. That's probably a good sign.

## What You'll Find Here

This space will be home to:

- **Race Reports** - Honest recaps of races I run, from short distances to longer efforts as I build toward that marathon goal.

- **Training Notes** - What's working, what's not, and what I'm learning about consistency, recovery, pacing, and patience.

- **Gear & Tools** - Straightforward thoughts on shoes, watches, and gear that actually earn their miles.

- **The Running Life** - The mental side of training—the motivation dips, the small wins, and everything that happens between start and finish.

## Why Write About Running?

Running is simple, but it isn't easy. Every run tells a story if you're paying attention. Writing helps me slow down and understand what those miles are teaching me—about discipline, resilience, and showing up when progress feels invisible.

If sharing that process helps someone else feel less alone in their own journey, even better. But first and foremost, this is about accountability and honesty—with myself and with the goal I'm chasing.

## The First Run

I didn't want this to just be words on a screen. So tonight, I laced up and went out for my first run of the year—an easy two miles. Nothing fancy. No pace goals. Just a deliberate start.

It felt exactly like it should: a little awkward, a little heavy, but full of intention. The kind of run that doesn't impress anyone, but matters more than it looks like it does. Two miles to say this is happening.

## Let's Get Moving

If you're a seasoned runner, a beginner, or somewhere in between, I'm glad you're here. This isn't about perfection or finish times—it's about forward motion.

Here's to starting where we are, trusting the process, and stacking miles—on the road and on the page.

See you out there.
